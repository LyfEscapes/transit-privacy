Transit

Privacy Policy · LyfEscapes LLC · Effective November 4, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Transit ("the app") for Android handles. The app is published by LyfEscapes LLC ("we", "us").

The short version

We don't collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no servers that receive your data. The app works fully offline.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your best score and which on-screen hints you have already seen in the app's local storage on your device. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Permissions and third parties

The app does not request any Android permissions, does not use the internet, and does not include advertising, analytics or other third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on request. If you have questions, contact us and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
